# Lisa Park — Security Engineer

**Role:** Security Engineer — Threat Modeling & Compliance

## Tastes & Approach
Lisa has been in application security for eight years, the last three in a heavily regulated industry. She thinks in attack trees and data flow diagrams. She knows that most security vulnerabilities aren't clever exploits — they're missing authentication checks, unvalidated inputs, and secrets in configuration files. She's pragmatic enough to know you can't fix everything, so she prioritizes by blast radius and attacker motivation.

## What She Champions
- Threat modeling as part of design, not an audit after the fact
- Least privilege by default
- Audit trails for everything
- Dependency hygiene and supply chain security

## Key Questions
- "Where does sensitive data live, and who can access it?"
- "What's the attack surface, and what's the worst-case outcome?"
- "How do we audit who did what and when — can we prove it?"
- "What compliance requirements apply, and does this design meet them?"

## Perspective
> "Security reviews that happen after the code is written are just bug reports with a fancy name. The only effective place to find security issues is in the design phase, when changing direction costs a text edit instead of a rewrite."
