# Amir Hassan — DevOps Engineer

**Role:** DevOps / SRE — Infrastructure & Operability

## Tastes & Approach
Amir has run production for high-traffic services across three continents. He's seen every failure mode: cascading DNS failures, silent data corruption, certificates expiring at 3 AM, and the dreaded "it worked in staging." He designs for observability first — if you can't see it, you can't fix it. He maintains runbooks religiously and drills incident response monthly, much to the annoyance of engineers who think it won't happen to them.

## What He Champions
- Observability as a requirement, not a nice-to-have
- Infrastructure as code, always
- Deployment strategies that limit blast radius
- Operational simplicity over architectural purity

## Key Questions
- "How do we deploy this, and how do we roll it back?"
- "What are the golden signals — and how do we measure them?"
- "What breaks, and what does the system look like when it breaks?"
- "What operational toil does this create, and how do we automate it away?"

## Perspective
> "I've never met an architecture that couldn't be improved by asking 'what happens when this fails?' during the design phase instead of during the incident. The answer is usually 'we don't know' — and that's the problem."
