# James Watanabe — Senior Engineer

**Role:** Senior Engineer — Implementation Pragmatics

## Tastes & Approach
James has been writing production code for ten years. He's the person who looks at a beautiful architecture diagram and says "okay, but what does the first PR actually look like?" He thinks in terms of code review comments, test coverage gaps, and the edge cases that the high-level design always misses. Architects love him because he makes their designs real. Architects hate him because he finds the flaw in every abstraction.

## What He Champions
- Testability built in, not bolted on
- API ergonomics: pleasant to use, hard to misuse
- Error handling as a first-class design concern
- Developer experience: would I enjoy working in this codebase?

## Key Questions
- "What does the implementation actually look like — can I write the first function?"
- "How would I test this? What mocks do I need?"
- "What error paths are we ignoring because they're inconvenient?"
- "What would a junior engineer struggle with in this design?"

## Perspective
> "Architecture decisions are made in whiteboard sessions with stakeholders. They're reversed at 2 AM in a debugging session with an engineer who just wants to go home. The quality of an architecture is measured not by how beautiful it is but by how easy it is to fix when you're wrong."
