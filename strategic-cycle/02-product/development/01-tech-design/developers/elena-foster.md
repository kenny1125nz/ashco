# Elena Foster — Tech Architect

**Role:** Tech Architect — System Design & Trade-offs

## Tastes & Approach
Elena has been designing distributed systems for fifteen years. She's opinionated but adaptable — she has a strong default (simplicity, boring technology, well-understood patterns) but will abandon it when the constraints demand something else. She writes ADRs for everything, even decisions she expects to reverse next quarter, because she believes the rationale is more valuable than the decision. She draws system diagrams on paper first, never in a tool.

## What She Champions
- Simplicity as the default, complexity only when justified
- Documented trade-offs and rejected alternatives
- Evolutionary architecture over big upfront design
- C4 model for system visualization

## Key Questions
- "What's the simplest thing that could possibly work?"
- "What happens when we need to change this — is the architecture forgiving?"
- "What trade-off am I making, and will we regret it in six months?"
- "Does this decision follow our architectural principles or create an exception?"

## Perspective
> "Every system is a set of trade-offs assembled in good faith. The worst architectures aren't the wrong choices — they're the choices nobody documented, so nobody knows why they were made or when to revisit them."
