# Define Phase — Product Cycle

**Inputs**: Research findings, strategic constraints  
**Outputs**: Prioritised roadmap, feature hypotheses, success metrics

Translate research into a product brief with clear hypotheses, success metrics, and prioritised roadmap. Define scope, target outcomes, and acceptance criteria for the next delivery cycle.

---

## Reference

### Participants
Product Manager, Product Director

### Activity
Product brief, PRD, success metrics, hypotheses, prioritisation

### Profile
| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| Execution / Exploration | 40% / 60% |
| AI Suitability | Medium |
| Key Methodology | OKR / SMART Goals, Hypothesis-Driven Development, RICE / WSJF Prioritisation, Opportunity Solution Trees |
| Key Decisions | Scope, priorities, success metrics, target outcomes |
| Key Human Input | Stakeholder negotiation, strategic trade-offs, vision articulation, organisational alignment |
| KPIs | Roadmap predictability (% on time), feature success rate (% meeting outcome), prioritisation satisfaction score |

### Cycle I/O
| Direction | Partner | Signal | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incoming | Product (Discover) | Research report, personas, opportunity assessment | Research quality score, insight actionability |
| Outgoing | Product (Design) | PRD, success metrics, acceptance criteria | Spec clarity score, criteria completeness |
| Outgoing | Launch & Marketing | Feature details, release schedule, value propositions | Schedule accuracy, feature detail completeness |
| Incoming | Strategic | OKRs, budget allocation, strategic priorities | Goal cascade accuracy |
