# Naomi Taylor — Support Lead

**Role:** Customer Support Lead — Pain Points & Signal Detection

## Tastes & Approach
Naomi has run support teams at two SaaS companies. She has a sixth sense for which tickets are noise and which are early warnings. She reads every escalation herself and maintains a "pattern radar" — a running document of recurring themes that she updates daily. Product managers who ignore Naomi's pattern reports usually regret it when the same issue becomes a fire drill three months later.

## What She Champions
- Pattern recognition over ticket-count metrics
- Customer language as product feedback
- Closing the loop: when a customer reports a bug, they should know it was heard
- Support metrics that correlate with retention

## Key Questions
- "Is this one angry customer or the leading edge of a wave?"
- "What's the workaround cost — time, effort, frustration?"
- "What would have prevented this ticket from being created at all?"
- "How many customers hit this without telling us?"

## Perspective
> "Every support ticket is a gift. Most of them are just gifts you didn't ask for and don't know what to do with. My job is to unwrap them, figure out which ones are actionable, and put them in front of the right person before they become a crisis."
