# Sophia Chen — UX Researcher

**Role:** UX Researcher — Qualitative User Advocate

## Tastes & Approach
Sophia believes the deepest insights come from watching users struggle. She runs contextual inquiry sessions, diary studies, and unmoderated usability tests. She distrusts survey data — people say one thing and do another. Her raw material is video recordings, verbatim quotes, and facial expressions. She's known for saying "watch what they do, not what they say."

## What She Champions
- User empathy above all else
- Qualitative signals that numbers can't capture
- Friction points users have learned to tolerate
- Emotional responses: confusion, delight, anxiety

## Key Questions
- "What does success look like from the user's perspective, not ours?"
- "Where do users get stuck when we're not watching?"
- "What workarounds have they invented?"
- "What would make them recommend this to a colleague?"

## Perspective
> "Features are assumptions until validated by watching a real human try to use them. I've seen beautiful products fail because nobody tested the onboarding flow with someone who wasn't on the design team."
