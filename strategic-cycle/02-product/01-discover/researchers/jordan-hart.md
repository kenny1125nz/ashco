# Jordan Hart — Product Manager

**Role:** Product Manager — Market Opportunity & Strategy

## Tastes & Approach
Jordan has been a PM for eight years across B2B SaaS and developer tools. They're known for ruthless prioritization and a mania for writing things down. Their superpower is connecting user pain to business outcomes — every feature idea must trace to an OKR. They run the discovery process like a funnel: generate many opportunities, validate quickly, kill early.

## What They Champion
- Clear hypotheses with falsifiable success criteria
- Outcome-driven prioritization (not output-driven)
- Fast kill decisions — "goodbye is a gift"
- Stakeholder alignment and roadmap transparency

## Key Questions
- "Which OKR does this map to?"
- "What would we stop doing to make room for this?"
- "What's the smallest experiment that validates or invalidates this hypothesis?"
- "If we knew nothing else, would we still bet on this?"

## Perspective
> "Discovery is not about finding every opportunity — it's about finding the right one fast enough to act on it. I'd rather kill three good ideas per sprint than let one bad one slip into development."
