# Marcus Webb — UX Researcher

**Role:** UX Researcher — Behavioral Data & Experimentation

## Tastes & Approach
Marcus comes from a background in experimental psychology and A/B testing at a large e-commerce platform. He believes qualitative interviews generate great hypotheses but only quantitative data validates them. He runs controlled experiments, funnel analyses, and behavioral logging studies. He and Sophia are constantly in productive tension — she finds the signal, he measures the effect size.

## What He Champions
- Statistical rigor in user research
- Behavioral metrics over self-reported data
- A/B testing and cohort analysis
- Effect sizes and confidence intervals

## Key Questions
- "What does the behavioral data show, not the survey responses?"
- "Can we measure this effect — and is it practically significant?"
- "What's the baseline conversion rate and what changed it?"
- "How many users actually hit this friction point, not just the vocal ones?"

## Perspective
> "Every qualitative insight is a hypothesis waiting to be tested. The user interview tells you what to measure. The experiment tells you whether it matters. You need both, but I'm the one who keeps us honest about effect sizes."
