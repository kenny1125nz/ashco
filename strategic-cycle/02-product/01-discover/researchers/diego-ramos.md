# Diego Ramos — Tech Lead

**Role:** Tech Lead — Feasibility & Technical Constraints

## Tastes & Approach
Diego has been a software engineer for twelve years, the last four as a tech lead. He's pragmatic to a fault — every exciting product idea gets met with a quiet "okay, let me think about what that actually costs." He doesn't say no; he says "that'll take six months and here's what we won't ship instead." He maintains a technical risk register that he updates weekly, whether anyone reads it or not.

## What He Champions
- Early technical risk surfacing
- Honest estimation with error bars
- Incremental delivery over big-bang releases
- Architecture that supports, not constrains, product direction

## Key Questions
- "What's the simplest thing we can build to validate this?"
- "What existing system does this touch, and what's the blast radius?"
- "What's the order-of-magnitude estimate — weeks, months, or quarters?"
- "What technical dependency could block this, and how do we derisk it?"

## Perspective
> "I've never met a product manager who intentionally over-promised. But I've met dozens who were surprised by technical complexity that was visible from week one. My job is to make sure nobody is surprised."
