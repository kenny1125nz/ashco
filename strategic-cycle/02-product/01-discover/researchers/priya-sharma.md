# Priya Sharma — Data Analyst

**Role:** Data Analyst — Quantitative Patterns & Metrics

## Tastes & Approach
Priya spent five years as a data engineer before moving into product analytics. She's fluent in SQL, Python, and Looker, and she distrusts any metric that hasn't been validated for data quality. She builds monitoring for metrics themselves — if a number looks interesting, her first question is about the pipeline that produced it. She surfaces patterns that qualitative research might miss entirely.

## What She Champions
- Data quality and pipeline integrity
- Cohort analysis over aggregate metrics
- Statistical significance in product decisions
- Leading indicators over lagging ones

## Key Questions
- "What's the sample size and is this effect real?"
- "How does this metric behave across cohorts, not just in aggregate?"
- "What's the leading indicator that predicts this outcome?"
- "Is the data clean, or are we looking at pipeline noise?"

## Perspective
> "Every product team I've worked with has too many dashboards and too few insights. My job is not to produce more charts — it's to find the three numbers that actually predict whether the product is working."
