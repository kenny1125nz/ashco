role: review

Evaluate the quality of the strategic plan on a scale of 0-100.

Criteria:
- Clarity: Are vision, OKRs, and mandates clearly stated? (0-25)
- Coherence: Do OKRs align with vision and budget? (0-25)
- Actionability: Can sub-cycles execute against the mandates? (0-25)
- Risk: Is risk appetite realistic given market conditions? (0-25)

Output SCORE: <0-100> and FINDINGS: <specific improvements needed>
