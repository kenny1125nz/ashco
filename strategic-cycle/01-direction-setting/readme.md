# Direction Setting Phase — Strategic Cycle

**Inputs**: Market trends, financial reports, portfolio health  
**Outputs**: Strategic plan, funding decisions, OKRs, sub-cycle mandates

Set corporate vision, OKRs, budget allocation, and risk appetite for the upcoming period. Produce a strategic plan with clear sub-cycle mandates for Product, Launch & Marketing, and Operations.

---

## Reference

### Participants
Board of Directors, CEO, Exec Team

### Activity
Set corporate vision, mission, OKRs, budget allocation, risk appetite

### Profile
| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| Execution / Exploration | 20% / 80% |
| AI Suitability | Medium |
| Key Methodology | SWOT / PESTLE Analysis, OKR Setting, Scenario Planning, Blue Ocean Strategy |
| Key Decisions | Corporate vision, OKRs, risk appetite, budget allocation, sub-cycle mandates |
| Key Human Input | Vision articulation, value-based strategy trade-offs, risk appetite definition, long-horizon intuition |
| KPIs | OKR attainment rate, strategic forecast accuracy, sub-cycle delivery enablement |

### Cycle I/O
| Direction | Partner | Signal | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outgoing | Product | OKRs, budget allocation, constraints, strategic priorities | OKR alignment rate, signal clarity score |
| Outgoing | Launch & Marketing | Corporate direction, release cadence, target markets | Direction-to-campaign latency, brief completeness score |
| Outgoing | Operations | Support priorities, incident response SLAs | Priority clarity score |
| Incoming (External) | Financial markets, shareholders | Investor expectations, funding availability | Signal accuracy vs market realisation |
| Incoming (External) | Industry analysts, media | Market intelligence, regulatory changes | Intelligence relevance rate, early warning lead time |
