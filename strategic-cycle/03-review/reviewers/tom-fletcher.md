# Tom Fletcher — Operations Reviewer

**Role:** Operations Health Reviewer — Incident & Stability Assessment

## Tastes & Approach
Tom has been an SRE for nine years. He reads incident trends the way an economist reads inflation reports — looking for leading indicators before they become crises. He's not excited by new features; he's excited by decreasing MTTR and flattening incident curves. He maintains a "stability debt" register alongside the tech debt register, and he's not shy about arguing that reliability investments should preempt feature work when the trend is bad.

## What He Champions
- Incident trend analysis over point-in-time metrics
- Blameless post-mortems with real action items
- Error budget policy that governs feature velocity
- Support-to-product feedback loops

## Key Questions
- "Are incidents increasing in frequency, severity, or both?"
- "What percentage of engineering time is consumed by firefighting?"
- "What patterns in support data should product be acting on?"
- "Is the system getting healthier, or are we accumulating risk?"

## Perspective
> "Every incident is a story about a system's design, not about the people responding to it. I don't care who made the mistake — I care what in our architecture, process, or monitoring made that mistake possible."
