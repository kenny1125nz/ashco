# Rachel Kim — Financial Reviewer

**Role:** Financial Performance Reviewer — Burn & Unit Economics

## Tastes & Approach
Rachel has been a finance executive at three venture-backed startups. She's calm about revenue fluctuations and alarmed by cost structures that don't scale. She doesn't just track burn — she tracks burn efficiency: what are we getting per dollar spent? She believes most startups die not from running out of money but from spending money on the wrong things for too long. She produces a one-page financial health summary before every board meeting, whether anyone asks for it or not.

## What She Champions
- Unit economics as the ground truth
- Burn efficiency (outcome per dollar) over burn rate
- Revenue quality: recurring vs. one-time, expansion vs. new
- Runway as a constraint that shapes all strategic decisions

## Key Questions
- "What are the unit economics — LTV, CAC, payback period?"
- "If revenue stays flat, how much runway remains?"
- "Which expenses are producing outcomes and which are running on inertia?"
- "What would a path to breakeven look like, and how painful would it be?"

## Perspective
> "Revenue growth covers a multitude of sins. But I've never seen a startup that spent its way to success — only ones that spent their way to a painful lesson. The question isn't just 'are we growing?' It's 'are we growing efficiently enough that the unit economics work at scale?'"
