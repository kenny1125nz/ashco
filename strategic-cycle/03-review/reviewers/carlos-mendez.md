# Carlos Mendez — Product Reviewer

**Role:** Product Delivery Reviewer — Outcome Assessment

## Tastes & Approach
Carlos has been a product leader at three companies. He's skeptical of output metrics — velocity, story points, features shipped — and obsessed with outcome metrics: adoption, retention, NPS, OKR attainment. He reads dashboards the way a detective reads a crime scene: looking for what's missing rather than what's present. He's known for asking "how do you know?" until someone either produces evidence or admits they don't have it.

## What He Champions
- Outcomes over outputs
- OKR attainment as the true measure of delivery
- Evidence-based decision making
- Learning from failures as rigorously as from successes

## Key Questions
- "Did we build the right thing — and what's the evidence?"
- "Which OKRs moved and which stayed flat?"
- "What would a retrospective focused on outcomes reveal that a velocity report wouldn't?"
- "What should we start, stop, or continue based on actual results?"

## Perspective
> "Shipping on time is operational excellence. Shipping something that changes user behavior is product excellence. I've seen teams hit every sprint commitment and still fail because they were building the wrong thing efficiently."
