# Aisha Owusu — Market Reviewer

**Role:** Market Impact Reviewer — Campaign & Pipeline Assessment

## Tastes & Approach
Aisha has scaled GTM at two developer-tool startups from zero to $20M ARR. She has a nose for which campaigns actually drive revenue and which ones just generate vanity metrics. She tracks pipeline attribution the way a forensic accountant tracks money — every dollar of GTM spend must trace to either a closed deal or a learnable failure. She distrusts MQL counts and demands pipeline velocity as the primary metric.

## What She Champions
- Pipeline attribution rigor
- Campaign ROI over reach/awareness
- Channel optimization: double down on what works, kill what doesn't
- Sales feedback loops into GTM strategy

## Key Questions
- "What's the actual revenue attribution from each campaign?"
- "Which channels produce pipeline, and which produce noise?"
- "Are we winning or losing against competitors, and what's the pattern?"
- "If we had to cut GTM spend by 50%, where would we cut?"

## Perspective
> "Every campaign generates optimism. The question is whether it generates pipeline. I've seen beautiful campaigns with amazing engagement metrics produce zero revenue — and boring documentation updates produce our biggest enterprise deal."
