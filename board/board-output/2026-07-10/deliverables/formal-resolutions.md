# Formal Board Resolutions — 2026-07-10

**Authority:** These resolutions were duly adopted by the board of directors of Ash Corporation at a meeting held on July 10, 2026, at which a quorum was present and voting. They constitute the binding, legal authority for the company to execute the actions described below.

---

## A. Pricing Model

**1.** RESOLVED THAT the board adopts a usage-based consumption pricing model for Ash Cloud with 500 free execution steps per user per month, a Team tier at $50/user/month for unlimited steps, and an Enterprise tier at $5K–$25K/month, with all parameters implemented as configuration and subject to revision within 30 days of receiving data from 100 active users.

**2.** RESOLVED THAT the local CLI shall remain free under the MIT license with no registration requirement.

**3.** RESOLVED THAT instrumentation for usage data shall ship from day one across all tiers, and that enterprise governance features (SOC 2, RBAC, SSO) shall justify premium pricing.

---

## B. Licensing

**4.** RESOLVED THAT the board adopts MIT as the project license, contingent on Ash Cloud becoming the premium governed runtime with deterministic replay, policy-as-code, and procurement artifacts, enterprise governance infrastructure (SOC 2 Type II by Q2 2027, RBAC, SSO, on-prem/VPC) serving as the primary defensibility, user-authorized telemetry shipping within two weeks, and the license decision not being revisited for 18 months or until 1,000 active developers publish workflows, whichever occurs later.

**5.** RESOLVED THAT only OSI-approved licenses are acceptable for the project, as any non-OSI license is a procurement disqualifier in regulated enterprises.

---

## C. DSL Investment

**6.** RESOLVED THAT the board adopts markdown as the primary user surface effective immediately, the .ash DSL is reclassified as an internal intermediate representation maintained for correctness only (one engineer 20% time, no new syntax unless markdown provably cannot express the use case), the onboarding narrative changes from "learn Ash syntax" to "write a workflow in markdown — Ash handles the execution," the CEO converts three reference workflows from .ash to .md and ships a one-command quickstart template within one week, and adoption of 10 active .md workflow authors by August 10 is the binding metric against which the pivot's success is measured.

---

## D. Budget

**7.** RESOLVED THAT the board approves pursuit of SOC 2 Type II certification as a Q2 2027 target, with the final budget to be determined upon receipt of auditor quotes, and with authority to defer event-triggered feature development to fund certification.

**8.** RESOLVED THAT the board approves a budget and timeline for hiring a second engineer, and that this expenditure shall be prioritized before all other discretionary spending.

---

## E. Hiring

**9.** RESOLVED THAT the company shall hire a second engineer before making any other non-essential hires or capital expenditures.

---

## F. Policies and Governance

**10.** RESOLVED THAT the CEO must circulate financial reporting — including cash balance, burn rate, and runway — to the board 48 hours before each board meeting.

**11.** RESOLVED THAT the CEO shall shift his time from feature engineering to direct onboarding activities — including personal demos, tutorials, and hand-holding the first 10 users — until meaningful adoption data exists.

**12.** RESOLVED THAT SOC 2 planning shall be assigned a named owner with a defined quarterly milestone, replacing the current Q2 2027 aspiration with accountable project management.

**13.** RESOLVED THAT the board shall institute monthly operational check-ins of 60 minutes duration with half the board attending, effective immediately, with full board meetings remaining quarterly.

**14.** RESOLVED THAT all board pre-read materials must include current financial statements and adoption metrics, and that in-session discovery of such fundamentals shall not recur.

**15.** RESOLVED THAT the CEO shall deliver a written founder-resilience plan by the next board meeting, documenting backup and succession arrangements.

**16.** RESOLVED THAT the board shall establish a kill criterion — a specific date or metric at which problem-solution fit is deemed unproven — by the next board meeting, triggering either a pivot or a recommendation of capital return.

---

*These resolutions were adopted on July 10, 2026 and recorded by the board secretary.*
