Read tmp/prioritized-ideas.md and audit the quality of the prioritisation, not the ideas themselves.

Check:
- **Completeness** — are all ideas from ideas/ present?
- **Consistency** — are the criteria applied uniformly across all entries?
- **Discrimination** — are scores spread meaningfully, or is everything clustered around the same range?
- **Justification** — do the rationales explain the ranking convincingly, or are they vague?
- **Actionability** — could someone use this list to decide what to work on next?

Do not re-score individual ideas. Focus on whether the prioritisation work is thorough, consistent, and useful.
